How to use it

Start at the centre and work outwards.

The model is not a filing cabinet. Each segment answers a small number of questions, and each worksheet exists to force one of those questions to be answered in writing.

01

Define the customer

Everything outside the centre is a bet on who you are serving. Get that written down first — the other eight segments inherit it.

02

Open one segment

Pick the part of the business that is currently costing you the most sleep. Read the questions it is meant to answer before you touch a worksheet.

03

Fill it in with real numbers

Download the tool, complete it against your actual business, and keep it. A model is only useful once it disagrees with you.
